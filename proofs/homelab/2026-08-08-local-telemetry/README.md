## How the local estate runs it

The operator points the runner at the authoritative catalog, audit, exact binding directory, and a new workspace. The repository supplies every source-free policy, recipe, worker, review, and circulation mechanism.

```powershell
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass `
  -File scripts\RUN_HOMELAB_FACTORY.ps1 `
  -Catalog "<authoritative homelab.catalog.json>" `
  -Audit "<authoritative homelab.audit.json>" `
  -Bindings "<private binding directory>" `
  -Workspace "<new factory workspace>" `
  -Profile core `
  -Gpu 0,1,2
```

The runner executes every dependency-ready machine task and stops at a public-only human review directory. After the owner chooses, `scripts/earcrate_factory.py review` seals the judgment, creates the scoped update, resumes machine work, and emits the circulation packet.

## Authority boundary

The factory may create observations, candidates, derived artifacts, exact execution receipts, quality archives, review assignments, review ledgers, and bounded preference updates. It may not accept a provider, silently write canonical musical state, claim a general taste model, approve rights, release audio, publish media, or claim whole-Buffalo passage.

A provider refusal remains visible. A least-bad candidate may be rejected. The machine frontier reduces listening load; the owner remains the musical acceptance authority. Learning is not claimed until one review changes a later comparable route while unrelated organs remain bit-identical.
