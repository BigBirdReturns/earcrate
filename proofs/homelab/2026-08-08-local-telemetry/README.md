# EarCrate local-to-cloud telemetry handoff

This packet is the source-free cloud review surface for one real local EarCrate
estate and Homelab audit. It exists so GitHub-connected LLM conversations can
inspect the current provider arcade, blockers, tasks, and listening residue
without receiving source audio, authoritative private objects, credentials, or
absolute local paths.

## What actually ran

1. Durable EarCrate `main`, the hot cloud reference, and `origin/main` were
   reconciled at `675fc43bd01a86e7d8dbd43957451513082554ef`.
2. `python -m earcrate estate sweep` scanned four explicit EarCrate roots in
   evidence-hash mode and captured audio devices. It completed in 532 seconds.
3. The sealed estate inventory and rig were compiled through the production
   Homelab catalog, node, audit, campaign, store, doctor, and public-export
   commands.
4. Four locally supplied Beggin' MP3 candidates were hashed and measured without
   being moved or copied. Three 30-second private listening hypotheses were
   rendered locally. No media is included here.

separation, embeddings and fingerprints, stretch and rendering, DJ and crate
systems, service integrations, commercial comparators, and interoperability or
supply-chain standards. A target being installed or feasible is not adoption.

## Beggin' listening residue

See `beggin-audition-telemetry.json` for content identities, signal measurements,
transforms, and the operator's verdict. The important outcome is:

> All initial hypotheses kind of sucked but were music-adjacent. AUD-02 was the
> relative winner. Its ending "beggin' you" repetitions were all off.

That verdict rejects a single global tempo transform as a phrase-alignment
contract. AUD-03 instead maps the observed vocal-block endpoints and remains
pending human review. The private WAVs are not in this packet.

## How to inspect this PR

Start with:

1. this README;
2. `beggin-audition-telemetry.json`;
3. `manifest.json`;
4. the catalog, audit, and campaign projections under `projections/`.

Useful review questions:

- Which of the 32 feasible targets should enter the first real-fixture audition
  tournament, given that feasibility is not acceptance?
- Which provider organs are redundant, complementary, or valuable only as
  reference controls?
- What is the smallest lawful campaign that exercises separation, phrase timing,
  percussion transplantation, and human A/B review on the Beggin' specimen?
- Which blockers are actual missing assets or credentials, and which are merely
  missing current receipts?
- How should phrase-local vocal timing be represented without turning one
  observed Beggin' alignment into a universal contract?

LLM recommendations are review input only. Local execution receipts and human
listening remain acceptance authority.
