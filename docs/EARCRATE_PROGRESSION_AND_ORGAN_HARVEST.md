# EarCrate progression and organ harvest

This document is the canonical map of the project lineage. It exists to preserve useful organs from experiments without preserving every experimental branch as an active product line.

## Thesis

EarCrate is one musical organism:

```text
heterogeneous evidence
    -> observations with custody and evidence tier
    -> canonical musical understanding
    -> lawful adjacent moves and arrangement obligations
    -> several executable bodies
    -> live or offline performance
    -> signal evaluation and human review
    -> selective recomputation and campaign evolution
```

An organ may prove one capability. Only the circulation among organs may prove the thesis.

## Evidence ladder

Evidence routes remain separate until a declared convergence step:

```text
authoritative score
    -> Children score control

community-symbolic witness
    -> Flim target-conditioned trio witness and continuation

blind audio inference
    -> Pretty Lights recurrence and source-transport specimens

cross-modal accepted state
    -> sealed convergence of independently produced branches

performance realization
    -> exact MIDI, racks, SourcePhrases, MixScore, live runtime

human review and campaign evidence
    -> ReviewPatch, release governance, changed future behavior
```

A community transcription may support a playable witness; it may not claim blind listening. A score-derived render may support execution; it may not enter an audio-inference branch. A clean signal may qualify a candidate; it may not become human musical acceptance.

## Directional invariants

1. **One organism, many bodies.** A single accepted musical intent may lower into MIDI, racks, SourcePhrases, MixScore, or live execution. Those bodies remain accountable to the same obligations.
2. **Evidence tiers never collapse silently.** Score, symbolic, audio, convergence, performance, review, and evolution retain separate ancestry.
3. **Providers propose; EarCrate adjudicates.** External organs may emit observations, candidates, measurements, refusals, artifacts, or unapplied patches. They may not write canonical musical state.
4. **Similarity is not fungibility.** A replacement must satisfy a PhraseContract covering role, time, transformations, identity, future obligations, and rights assertions.
5. **Signal sanity is not musical acceptance.** Builders and signal evaluators cannot approve their own music.
6. **Release eligibility is not publication.** Publication requires an exact content-bound permit and an atomic publication receipt.
7. **No parallel product lanes.** New experiments enter as providers, specimens, campaigns, or bounded organs under the Floor, not as replacement application stacks.
8. **Superseded scaffolds close promptly.** Temporary export workflows, overlay payloads, and bootstrap-only PRs are not permanent architectural records.
9. **Source media stays external by default.** Repository fixtures retain identities, manifests, ledgers, schemas, builders, and receipts rather than copyrighted or private media.
10. **Human correction must change future behavior.** Review is not complete until a correction creates a child revision, invalidates only dependents, and can alter later ranking or policy.

## Governance v2 hardening before merge

PR #49 is the canonical publication-governance child of PR #47, but it remains draft until these surfaces are complete:

- public review campaign separated from private assignment authority;
- per-reviewer randomized option mapping;
- assignment-map commitment included in the campaign identity;
- every review bound to campaign, candidate, control, policy, and assignment commitments;
- explicit authentication boundary for human reviewer identity;
- sealed arbitration path for split votes;
- versioned schemas, CLI, protocol documentation, and specimen fixture;
- format-neutral artifact roles rather than hard-coded MP3 filenames;
- atomic staged publication with fsync, rehash, rename, cleanup, and PublicationReceipt;
- rights use, jurisdiction, and expiry carried into the publish permit.

Until those conditions are met, PR #49 is an organ under development, not the release authority.
