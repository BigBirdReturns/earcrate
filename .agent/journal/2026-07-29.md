# 2026-07-29

## Reviewed release candidates — signal sanity is not musical acceptance

**State:** The Open Music Evidence Floor could custody provider evidence, source and
performance time, phrase contracts, independent evaluations, review patches, and
rights assertions. It did not yet define the promotion boundary between a rendered
audio candidate and music that EarCrate may call accepted. A conservative Empire State
recurrence edit demonstrated the missing shape: exact source-only construction,
automatic delivery gates, and an intentionally pending human verdict.

**Did:**

- Added `AudioEditPlan`, `ReleaseCandidate`, `SignalEvaluation`,
  `HumanMusicalReview`, and `ReleaseGateReceipt` authorities.
- Added sample-accurate source/output interval custody, explicit overlap transitions,
  declared/prohibited operations, and source-only enforcement.
- Refused candidate-builder self-approval, builder-as-signal-evaluator, machine output
  masquerading as human review, and rights assertions masquerading as legal decisions.
- Added a multidimensional release status vector instead of one overloaded approval
  flag.
- Required custody, two-run reproducibility, independent signal passage, human
  acceptance, and rights-policy acceptance before release promotion.
- Added package and single-file CLI commands for recurrence adaptation, review
  templates, and release-gate assembly.
- Added five schemas, an executable gap-register extension, documentation, and focused
  gates.
- Registered the source-free `pretty_lights_empire_release_candidate_v1` fixture and
  its exact external source/proof-pack identities.

**Fixture result:**

- 31.261-second source-only candidate;
- eight contiguous retained bars plus four bars from a non-overlapping recurrence;
- 35 ms equal-power transition;
- -9.019 LUFS and -0.500 dBFS 4x true peak;
- 0.993 chroma, 0.994 mel, and 0.890 onset recurrence similarity;
- two isolated builds with bit-exact PCM, WAV, MP3, 30-second MP3, metrics, and proof
  ZIP;
- signal status passed;
- human musical acceptance pending;
- rights eligibility not evaluated;
- release blocked.

**Boundary:** A release gate does not imply Buffalo Gate passage. Human review remains
an explicit authority, and rights-policy acceptance remains an assertion/policy
decision rather than a legal determination.
