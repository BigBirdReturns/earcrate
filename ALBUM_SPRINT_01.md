# Album One Sprint 01

Album One advances seven track lanes in parallel. A proving track may exercise reusable infrastructure, but it may not block the other commissioned tracks.

## Owner-facing rule

Machine diagnostics may be short. Owner-facing audio is a musical proposition, not a unit test. Except where the musical object is inherently short, a frontier must contain setup, body, and payoff or release.

Each track receives at most one owner frontier in this sprint, with at most four cuts. The musical delta between cuts must be disclosed before listening. Provider identity may be blinded when useful; the musical operation may not. A frontier whose cuts share one dominant audible defect is invalid and requires machine iteration rather than an owner ranking.

## Sprint exit

Every track must reach one of two states:

1. machine-qualified full-form owner frontier; or
2. exact irreducible blocker with the complete runnable contract already prepared.
