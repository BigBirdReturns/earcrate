## Status vocabulary

- **Main canon:** reachable from the audited `main`.
- **Pending canon:** implemented and verified in an open PR, but not yet product
  authority.
- **External evidence canon:** retained by exact identity outside the repository.
- **Conversation-reported evidence:** preserved honestly, but not durable custody.
- **Harvested canon:** the invariant survived through another implementation.
- **Deferred canon:** intentional direction without a current implementation.
- **Retired:** deliberately rejected authority or delivery mechanism.
- **Failed delivery:** a bootstrap/export/transport failed; it is not an organ.
- **Corrected claim:** an earlier overstatement retained with its correction.

> Nothing disappears because it missed `main`, and nothing becomes product truth
> merely because we once described it confidently.

## Head canon

1. EarCrate is one organism that circulates evidence through observation,
   musical understanding, lawful choice, multiple execution bodies, review,
   selective recomputation, and changed future behavior.
2. Score, community-symbolic, blind PCM, convergence, performance, review, and
   campaign evidence retain separate ancestry.
3. The cephalopod reads before the player piano composes.
4. A sample library is not a mashup performance; independent source transports
   and mixer state are required.
5. Two decks are the metaphor; N decks are the internal engine.
6. Similarity is not fungibility. `PhraseContract` owns slot admissibility.
7. Providers propose; EarCrate adjudicates.
8. No organ-level success is whole-Buffalo passage.
9. Signal sanity is not musical acceptance.
10. Review is not learning until it changes a later decision.
11. Every source, event, transform, execution, and refusal is accounted for.
12. Source media stays external by default.
13. Buy the commodity; own the score, custody, obligations, and receipts.
14. No rescue, degraded, hidden, or silent fallback.

## Update rule

A future change may not silently delete or rewrite canon. It must identify the
prior entry, append a correction or new revision, explain why, preserve evidence
identity and tier, record open debt, recompute the effective ledger hash, and
pass `tests/test_canon_ledger.py`.
